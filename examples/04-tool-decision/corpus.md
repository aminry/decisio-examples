# Northwind Support handbook (fictional, written for this example)

Refunds: annual plans can be refunded in full within 30 days of purchase, and pro rata after that for the unused months.
Support hours: weekdays 08:00 to 18:00 Central European Time; on 24 and 31 December, 08:00 to 13:00; closed on 25 and 26 December.
Plans: the Team plan allows up to 25 seats; the Business plan allows up to 500 seats and includes single sign-on.
Data location: customer data is hosted in Frankfurt and Dublin; customers may choose the region when the workspace is created.
Data export: an admin requests an export under Settings, Data, and receives a download link within 24 hours.
Priority-one tickets: first response within 1 hour, around the clock, on the Business plan.
Browsers: the dashboard supports the two latest versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari and Edge.
Uploads: files up to 50 MB can be attached to a ticket.
